SEMANTIC REVIEW SCOPE
---------------------
The semantic interpretation was based on structured visual review of the
locked Grad-CAM++ atlas.

The annotations are visual-descriptive.

They were NOT validated by:
- an expert plant pathologist;
- lesion-segmentation ground truth;
- pixel-level disease masks.

The XAI cohort was deterministically stratified and therefore does not
represent an unbiased population sample.

INTERPRETATION:
Within this selected external XAI cohort, incorrect predictions were
associated with lower visible symptom-region alignment and considerably more
background/context attention.

This pattern is consistent with background/context sensitivity contributing
to some external-domain failures.

It does NOT establish background bias as a causal explanation for the overall
PlantDoc performance collapse.


2. MISCLASSIFICATION IS NOT ONLY A LOCALIZATION FAILURE
-------------------------------------------------------

Several external wrong predictions still had STRONG or PARTIAL
symptom-region alignment.

Therefore, spatial localization alone cannot explain all errors.

The model can attend to visibly abnormal leaf tissue yet still assign the
wrong disease class.

This is consistent with a second failure mode involving disease-class
discrimination under domain shift.

INTERPRETATION:
Changing the Grad-CAM++ target from the erroneous predicted class to the
ground-truth class frequently changes the spatial evidence used by the
network.

In several cases, the true-class CAM shifts toward relevant leaf/symptom
regions or away from background/context.

The pattern is not universal; some cases remain unchanged and some become
less symptom aligned.

FINAL XAI CONCLUSION
--------------------

The locked XAI evidence is consistent with multiple simultaneous
external-domain failure mechanisms:

1. increased background/context attention in many selected external errors;

2. weak or absent symptom localization in some external errors;

3. incorrect disease discrimination despite plausible symptom-focused
   attention in other errors;

4. different spatial evidence for competing predicted and ground-truth
   disease classes;

5. highly confident predictions that remain incorrect and sometimes
   semantically poorly localized.

These findings complement the severe PlantVillage-to-PlantDoc performance
and calibration degradation identified previously.

They must be reported as descriptive XAI evidence, not causal proof.


LIMITATIONS
-----------

- Deterministically stratified XAI cohort.
- Small selected subgroup counts.
- No lesion segmentation masks.
- No expert pathology annotation validation.
- Grad-CAM++ is post-hoc.
- No population-level inference.
- No causal background-bias claim.
