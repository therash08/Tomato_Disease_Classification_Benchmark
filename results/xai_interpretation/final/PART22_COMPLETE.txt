INTERPRETATION
--------------

The selected external wrong cases exhibit lower visible symptom-region
alignment and substantially greater background/context concern than selected
external correct cases.

However, some incorrect predictions remain symptom aligned, demonstrating
that external-domain errors cannot be attributed only to spatial
mislocalization.

Predicted-class and ground-truth-class Grad-CAM++ maps often rely on
different spatial evidence.

In multiple external errors, changing the target to the true class reduces
background attention or moves attention toward relevant plant/symptom tissue.

High prediction confidence does not guarantee correct classification or
semantically appropriate attention under external domain shift.
