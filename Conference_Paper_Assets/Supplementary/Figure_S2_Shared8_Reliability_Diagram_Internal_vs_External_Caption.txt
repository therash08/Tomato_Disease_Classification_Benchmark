Supplementary Figure S2. Reliability comparison between the strict shared-eight PlantVillage internal test subset and the PlantDoc external test set using the frozen Improved ConvNeXt-Tiny. The internal evaluation showed near-perfect calibration (ECE = 0.000558), whereas the external evaluation showed marked miscalibration (ECE = 0.548602) despite a mean prediction confidence of 92.83% and an accuracy of 37.97%. The figure is reproduced byte-identically from the locked PART 20 analysis; no post-hoc calibration was applied.
