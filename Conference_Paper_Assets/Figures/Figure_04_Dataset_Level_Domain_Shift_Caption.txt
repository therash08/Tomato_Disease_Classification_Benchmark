Figure 4. Quantitative comparison of low-level image characteristics between the untouched PlantVillage internal test set (n = 2,399) and the independent PlantDoc external test set (n = 79). Boxplots summarize brightness, contrast, sharpness measured by variance of the Laplacian, grayscale entropy, mean RGB intensities, image dimensions, and aspect ratio. Cohen's d reports the standardized mean difference computed as PlantDoc minus PlantVillage. The largest absolute standardized differences were Aspect Ratio (d = +4.62); Image Width (d = +3.69); Image Height (d = +3.65). These measurements provide descriptive evidence of dataset-level covariate differences and are not interpreted as establishing a causal mechanism for the observed external-performance degradation.
