Figure 5. Principal component analysis of frozen penultimate ConvNeXt-Tiny representations for the independent PlantVillage internal test set (n = 2,399) and the PlantDoc external test set (n = 79). PCA was fitted jointly to the 768-dimensional frozen embeddings after mean centering, without per-feature standardization or model adaptation. PC1 and PC2 explained 19.40% and 15.47% of total embedding variance, respectively (34.87% cumulative). The standardized domain shifts were d = +0.14 on PC1 and d = -0.72 on PC2. The observed distributional separation is interpreted as descriptive evidence of representation-level domain shift and does not establish a causal explanation for external classification errors.
