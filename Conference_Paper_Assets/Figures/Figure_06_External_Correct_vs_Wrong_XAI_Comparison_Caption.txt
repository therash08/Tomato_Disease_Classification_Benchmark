Figure 6. Grad-CAM++ comparison of selected external correct and incorrect predictions from the frozen Improved ConvNeXt-Tiny. Within the deterministic XAI cohort, symptom-aligned attention was observed in 80.00% of applicable external correct cases and 40.00% of applicable external incorrect cases, whereas background/context concern was observed in 27.27% and 70.59%, respectively. These findings describe the selected explanation cohort only. Grad-CAM++ is a post-hoc attribution method, no expert lesion segmentation masks were available, and the observed attention patterns are not interpreted as causal explanations of model error or as population-level prevalence estimates.
