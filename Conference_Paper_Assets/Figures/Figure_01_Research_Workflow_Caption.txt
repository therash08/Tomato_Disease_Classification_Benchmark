Figure 1. Overall experimental workflow. The PlantVillage tomato dataset was subjected to image-quality verification, duplicate auditing, leakage-controlled group-aware splitting, and transfer-learning benchmarking across six pretrained architectures. Improved ConvNeXt-Tiny was selected using validation performance and subsequently frozen before independent internal and PlantDoc external evaluation. External degradation was characterized using class-wise performance analysis, dataset- and feature-space domain-shift analyses, confidence/calibration assessment, and Grad-CAM++ interpretation. No external fine-tuning or test-driven model optimization was performed.
